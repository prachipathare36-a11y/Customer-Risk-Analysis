# 📊 Customer Risk Analysis Dashboard

## 1. Project Preview

![Customer Risk Analysis Dashboard](Customer%20Risk%20Analysis.png)

The Customer Risk Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer churn and identify key factors that contribute to customer risk. The dashboard provides insights into customer behavior based on internet service, contract type, tenure, payment method, and monthly charges.



## 2. Key Features

- Interactive churn analysis using filters and slicers.
- Analysis of churn rate by internet service type.
- Customer distribution analysis by internet service.
- Analysis of monthly charges.
- Churn analysis based on contract type.
- Customer and churn analysis based on tenure.
- Churn analysis by payment method.
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for Total Customers, Churn Rate, Yearly Charges, Admin Tickets, and Tech Tickets.

# 6. Story

## 📌 About the Data

The dataset contains customer demographic, service, billing, contract, and churn-related information.

It includes customer details such as internet service, contract type, tenure, payment method, monthly charges, total charges, and support tickets.

The data is used to understand customer behavior and identify factors associated with customer churn.



## 🔍 Business Problem Analysis

### 1. Which Internet Service Type Has the Highest Customer Churn?

The analysis compares churn rates across different internet service types to identify which group has the highest risk of leaving.

### 2. Which Contract Type Has the Highest Customer Churn?

The analysis evaluates customer churn across different contract types to understand how contract duration affects customer retention.

### 3. Which Payment Method Has the Highest Customer Churn?

The analysis compares churn rates across payment methods to identify payment categories associated with higher customer churn.



## 📈 Key Conclusions

### 1. Fiber Optic Customers Have the Highest Churn Rate

Customers using Fiber Optic internet service have the highest churn rate at **41.89%**, indicating a higher risk of customer loss within this service category.

### 2. Month-to-Month Contract Customers Have the Highest Churn Rate

Customers with a Month-to-Month contract have a churn rate of **42.71%**, suggesting that customers with shorter contract commitments are more likely to leave.

### 3. Electronic Check Users Have the Highest Churn Rate

Customers using the Electronic Check payment method have the highest churn rate at **45.29%**, making this customer group a significant area for retention analysis.



## 📊 Overall Dashboard Insight

The overall customer churn rate is **26.54%**.

The dashboard helps identify high-risk customer segments and supports data-driven decision-making for improving customer retention strategies.
